# Operational formulas

## Sales and gross-to-net
Issued sales = sum of issued line quantities × final unit prices (or authoritative stored line totals under existing rounding rules). Net sales = issued sales + signed posted revenue adjustments. Gross/list sales and off-invoice discounts may be shown separately only when historical list prices and discount amounts are captured; otherwise label gross-to-net detail unavailable. Never subtract a discount already included in final unit price. A refund alone does not change sales. Do not classify all credits as product returns.

## Collections and aging
Net collected = receipts + replacement receipts − refunds − correction reversals; count each ledger entry exactly once. Per invoice outstanding = max(adjusted total − net collected, 0); overpayment = max(net collected − adjusted total, 0). Aggregate separately. Paid if outstanding = 0, partial if outstanding > 0 and net collected > 0, unpaid if outstanding > 0 and net collected = 0; flag negative net or unsupported negative invoice total for reconciliation. Overdue if positive outstanding and effective due date before Baghdad report date. Suggested aging buckets: not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, >90 days, unknown due date. Reminder due: active reminder scheduled at or before as-of timestamp, with outstanding > 0; sum each invoice once. Historical aging requires historical due dates and ledger state, otherwise disclose limitation.

## Product and outlet performance
Net units = issued quantities + verified signed quantity adjustments, not monetary credits alone. Use consistent comparable units per SKU; do not sum incompatible packs or units without conversion. Adjusted COGS = issued quantity × historical cost snapshot + verified signed cost-impact adjustments. Gross profit = net sales − adjusted COGS; gross margin = gross profit / net sales for positive sales only. For price-only credits COGS is unchanged; returns may reverse cost only when verified. No allocation of non-itemized adjustments across SKUs unless explicit approved allocation. Report unallocated amounts and coverage. Client gross profit is not full client profitability. Rank by absolute gross profit and margin where complete; show data coverage. SKU sales velocity = comparable net units / calendar days in selected period; not inventory turnover or sell-through. Average selling price = attributable net sales / net units when positive and comparable. Customer concentration = outlet net sales / overall net sales when denominator positive. Growth = (current − prior)/prior when prior positive; use matched periods. Average invoice value uses same invoice cohort numerator and denominator. Visit conversion = distinct eligible visits with issued orders / distinct eligible visits; exclude canceled empty starts and show started/completed separately. Undefined ratios are null, never zero by default.
